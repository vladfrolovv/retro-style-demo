The idea

The foam that saves you also knocks you down.

A one-room first-person game. You carry a heavy foam gun and put out the fires. But every spray makes the floor slippery: for you, the furniture and your partner.

  • Heavy foam gun

    Two hands, a kick when the pressure builds, and a tank you have to refill.

  • Foam is slippery

    Foam on the floor kills your grip. Brake late and you slide. Your partner slips too, and blames you.

  • Fire that spreads

    Heat jumps to anything that burns. Foam cools a fire and keeps it out for a while.

  • Save the plant

    Put out every fire, then carry the flower pot out of the room.

How I built it

  • Claude + Codex AI agents for the code, the Blender scripts and the art.
  • Blender vibe modeling: models, rigs and animation.
  • Unity / URP + Zenject gameplay, physics and light; MVC with dependency injection.
  • UI Toolkit the HUD, the menus and the rest of the UI.
